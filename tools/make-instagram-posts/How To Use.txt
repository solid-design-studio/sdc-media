MAKE INSTAGRAM POSTS

Turns an approved presentation into designed Instagram posts (1080 x 1350)
in Illustrator, exported as 300 ppi JPGs. Nothing to install. Your
presentation file is never changed.

The tool does not copy the slides. It takes each slide apart and lays the
pieces out again for Instagram:
- Every mockup, photo and logo gets its own post, whole. Nothing is cut in two.
- The text is set again, at one size across the deck, aligned the way it was
  written (left, centred, or right for Arabic).
- A slide with one mockup and a little text becomes one post, text above it.
- Each post keeps the slide's background, or the coloured panel the piece
  sat on.
- Every post has the same margins.
- Page numbers, corner logos and small slide captions are left out.


EVERY PROJECT

1. Open the approved presentation (.ai) in Illustrator.

   If Illustrator says fonts are missing: install them from the designer's
   files first (handovers have a Fonts folder), then open the presentation
   again. Otherwise some text can show wrong in the posts.
   If Illustrator says "Could not find the linked file": tick "Apply to All",
   then click Ignore. The tool will tell you which slides are missing a photo.

2. In the menu bar, click File > Scripts > Other Script...

3. Go to Dropbox > Nardeen > SOLAI > Tools > Make-Instagram-Posts,
   click "Make Instagram Posts.jsx", click Open.
   (After the first time, Illustrator opens this folder for you.)

4. A window called "Make Instagram Posts" appears. It lists every slide, how
   many posts it will make, and what it will become:
   - Designed: each asset on its own post, the text set again. This is the
     default.
   - Whole slide, shrunk to fit: keeps the slide's layout, centred on its
     background.
   - Whole slide, filling the post: keeps the slide's layout, trims the sides.
   - Skip: leaves the slide out.

   You do not have to change anything. Slides marked "check" are worth a look
   once the posts are made; hold the mouse over "check" to see why.

5. Pick the export size (300 ppi is the default) and click "Make posts".
   A progress bar shows while it works.

6. A message tells you what was made and which posts to look at. Click OK.

   Next to your presentation you now have:
   - "<presentation name> Instagram.ai": the posts, one 1080 x 1350 artboard
     per post, each in its own layer. This is now the open window.
   - A folder with the same name: the JPGs, numbered in order, ready to upload.

   If the presentation's folder can't be saved to, both go on your Desktop
   instead, and the message says so.


CHANGING A POST

1. In the posts file, double-click the post to go inside it.
2. Move or scale the artwork. Hold Shift while scaling to keep its shape.
   Press Esc when done.
3. Run File > Scripts > Other Script... > "Make Instagram Posts.jsx" again.
   It sees this is a posts file. Click Export.
   It saves your changes and puts the new JPGs in a folder ending in
   "updated", next to the posts file.


WHAT "CHECK" MEANS

- background photo trimmed a lot: a wide photo was cropped to fill the tall
  post. Double-click the post and slide the photo to show the best part.
- photo trimmed to fill the post: the same, but only a little was trimmed.
- a lot of empty space around the asset: the asset is very wide or very
  small, so it leaves room around it. Scale it up or switch the slide to
  "Whole slide".
- image enlarged a lot: the photo may look soft. Ask for a larger file.
- a long line of text was set smaller to fit: check it is still easy to read.
- detailed pattern was scaled: check the pattern still looks right.
- missing image: the designer's handover is missing a linked photo. Ask for
  the Links folder, put it next to the presentation, and run the tool again.


GOOD TO KNOW

- Running the tool twice on the same presentation never overwrites anything.
  The second set is numbered "Instagram 2".
- Decks with more than 120 posts are split into "Instagram part 1",
  "Instagram part 2", and so on.
- Keep at least 20 GB free on your Mac when working with large presentations.
