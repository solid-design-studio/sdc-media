# Soil hero film: source plan

Goal: use as little Veo as possible. Stock for real-world footage, motion graphics and renders for the zoom reveals and identity, Veo only where the story needs a specific action stock cannot give.

Stock titles below are from the Magnific (Freepik) stock library. Search the title to find the clip. Each premium clip costs 600 credits to license (the account currently has 28).

## Veo prompt (required)

**Shot 8 (8s, 16:9, 1080p):** Top-down shot of a light wooden table in soft morning light, a notebook on the left and a faint circular coffee ring mark on the right. A person's hand enters from the right and slides a white cup of coffee on a saucer across the table, setting it down exactly on the ring mark, then leaves frame. Steam rises from the cup. Quiet everyday moment, motion blur on the slide, no text, no logos.

## Veo prompt (optional, only if stock 7 feels generic)

**Shot 7 (6s):** Close-up of hands slowly pouring hot water from a gooseneck kettle into a ceramic pour-over dripper, coffee bed blooming and bubbling, steam in warm morning light, shallow depth of field, gentle motion blur, no text.

## Rules for the stock pick

- Pick clips that start or end on texture, so they cut into the zoom reveals.
- Grade everything to one warm, low-contrast look, then add motion blur on the cuts.
- Avoid clips with visible branding, faces or cafe interiors. The film stays on material and hands.
