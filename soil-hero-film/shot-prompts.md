# Soil hero film: shot list and Veo prompts

Campaign line: **السالفة أعمق**
Runtime of the mockup: 61.5 seconds, 16:9, 24 fps.

The mockup in this folder is a procedural animatic. It locks the structure, timing, camera moves, type and sound cues. The plates are generated stand-ins, not final footage. Each shot below has a prompt ready for Google Veo 3.1 (or Seedance in Magnific) so the stand-ins can be swapped for generated footage without changing the edit.

All AI shots: 16:9, 1080p, no on-screen text, no logos. Type, logo and the sand-layer identity build are finished in After Effects with the real identity files.

Motion blur is mandatory (brief). Ask for it in every prompt, then add more in the edit on the transitions.

Even, soft warm light. Slow exponential zoom out, motion blur during the move, no text.

**Prompt 3 (4s):** Aerial drone shot looking straight down over a coffee farm on a gentle hillside, curved rows of dark green coffee shrubs on red-brown earth, scattered shade trees, low morning sun. The camera glides forward quickly, strong directional motion blur at the start, no text.

**Prompt 4 (6s):** Extreme macro of a smooth glossy dark brown surface with a curved groove, unrecognisable at first. The camera pulls back steadily to reveal it is the centre crease of a single roasted coffee bean, then keeps pulling back until the bean sits among hundreds of roasted beans. Soft top light, glossy highlights, shallow depth of field, motion blur during the pull-back, no text.

**Prompt 5 (4s):** Close-up through the glass window of a commercial coffee roaster drum, roasted beans tumbling and cascading inside, warm amber light from the heat, metal rim around the window. Fast motion with heavy motion blur on the beans, slow push in, no text.

**Prompt 6 (6s):** Top-down extreme macro of espresso crema slowly swirling in a spiral, caramel and dark brown tones. The camera pulls straight up to reveal a white ceramic cup and saucer on a light wooden table. Soft daylight, slow rotation of the liquid, motion blur, no text.
